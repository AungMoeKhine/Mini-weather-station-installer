Quick Installation Guide
1
Connect your ESP32 CYD 2.8" board to your computer via USB using Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
2
Click Install Firmware below and select your board's serial port.
3
Download the required icon folders, copy them directly to the SD card's root directory, and insert the SD card into the slot.
